# KB-04: Helpdesk workflow

Document ID: KB-04
Version: v1
Effective date: 1 Sep 2026
Status: CURRENT

## Purpose

The helpdesk keeps a record of the question, known facts, evidence, unresolved items, and next action. Its goal is to help a coordinator make a traceable decision. A case may contain several questions, such as certificate eligibility and an extension request. Explain each issue separately rather than hiding a pending issue behind one overall positive answer.

## Open

Use Open when a case has been received and is being assessed. Save the learner name, question, supplied facts, and the latest analysis. Unknown facts should be identified clearly. The app should remain useful even when the LLM is temporarily unavailable.

## Waiting for Learner

Use this status when the learner must supply missing information, attendance evidence, a README, an accessible project link, or a corrected submission. State exactly what the learner needs to provide. A vague message such as "please clarify" is less useful than requesting the missing attendance record or mentor approval evidence.

## Waiting for Mentor

Approval-dependent questions go to Waiting for Mentor. Typical examples are a permitted makeup request or a timely extension awaiting approval. State that the decision is pending. Do not mark an extension as granted or a makeup session as completed merely because the request has been escalated.

## Ready for Review

Use Ready for Review when the necessary facts and a safe submission are available for a human reviewer. This status is not itself a certificate or an approval. A reviewer still needs to confirm the relevant result and complete the decision.

## Closed

Use Closed only when the coordinator has completed the case and recorded the outcome. A completed outcome may be a confirmed decision or a resolved explanation. Do not automatically close a case that still has unanswered questions, missing facts, or pending approval.

## Multiple blockers

When a case needs both learner evidence and mentor approval, record both next actions. For this assessment, one displayed status is enough: either waiting status is acceptable if the explanation makes both blockers visible. You do not need to implement parallel approval queues or a complex state machine.

## Minimum case note

Keep a concise note containing the learner facts, source IDs and versions used, outcome or uncertainty, unresolved items, and next action. A response should explain why the current evidence supports the recommendation. Internal model reasoning or a hidden chain of thought is not required.

## Answer outside the policies

If the question concerns fees, employment offers, stipend payments, or another topic absent from the pack, say that the available policies do not establish the answer. Refer the issue to the coordinator. Do not invent policy, costs, contact details, or service-level promises.
