# KB-02: Capstone policy

Document ID: KB-02
Version: v3
Effective date: 1 Oct 2026
Status: CURRENT

## Published deadline and pass score

The capstone is due Friday, 9 Oct 2026 at 17:00 PKT. The passing score is at least 70/100. A score of 70 meets the threshold; 69 does not. A score of 65 cannot be treated as a pass using the archived 60-point rule. A missing score means the capstone has not yet been confirmed as passed.

## Permitted extension

A one-business-day extension may be granted only when the learner requests it before the published deadline and a mentor approves it. Both conditions are required. A request alone is not approval. The assistant must distinguish not requested, requested but pending, approved, and denied where those facts are available.

## Meaning of one business day

For this fictional assessment, business days are Monday through Friday and there are no holidays. An approved one-business-day extension from Friday, 9 Oct at 17:00 PKT ends Monday, 12 Oct 2026 at 17:00 PKT. It does not end on Saturday. Use PKT consistently when interpreting the supplied case times.

## Unresolved request details

If the request time is missing, do not claim the request was made before the deadline. Ask for the timestamp or explain the uncertainty. If a request was timely but mentor approval is pending, keep the standard due date in force and identify the approval as outstanding. Do not automatically change a due date based on a learner asking for more time.

## After-deadline requests

This policy does not authorize an automatic extension for a request made at or after the deadline. Refer such a case for human review without promising that it will be accepted. Do not invent a second extension, a grace period, or an alternative pass threshold. Passing numerical scores and submission acceptance are separate facts.

## Approval evidence

An approved extension should be supported by a recorded mentor decision. A learner saying that a colleague thinks an extension is available does not establish approval. The recommendation should state the current due date, the unresolved approval if any, and the person who needs to act next.

## Version conflict

KB-02 v3 supersedes KB-05 v1 for cases on or after 1 Oct 2026. The archived score and deadline must not justify a current decision. Explain the conflict in plain language and cite the current version. Keep the archive searchable for historical explanation, but label it as superseded.

## Limits of this source

The policy specifies eligibility thresholds and a permitted extension, not a full academic appeal process. When a question asks about a rule not provided here, state that the source does not cover it and recommend coordinator or mentor clarification.
