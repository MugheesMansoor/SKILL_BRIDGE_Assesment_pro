# KB-01: Attendance and certification

Document ID: KB-01
Version: v2
Effective date: 1 Sep 2026
Status: CURRENT

## Purpose and scope

This policy applies to learners enrolled in the SkillBridge training program. It explains how attendance, live-session participation, and the capstone combine to determine certificate eligibility. Coordinators use recorded facts and verified evidence. Learner statements are useful for identifying an issue, but do not replace the official record.

## Certificate requirements

A learner must have attendance of at least 80%, participation in at least 3 live sessions, and a passing capstone under the current capstone policy. All three conditions are required. A valid, safe-to-review submission is also required under KB-03. Meeting attendance requirements alone does not establish that the learner passed the program. If a capstone score is unknown, certification remains pending.

## Attendance values

Attendance is recorded as a percentage from 0 to 100. Compare the recorded value against 80 without rounding upward to create eligibility. A value of 79.9% is below the threshold; 80% meets it. Live sessions are counted as whole, completed sessions. An unknown percentage or session count must remain unknown, rather than being converted to zero or assumed sufficient.

## Medical absence and evidence

A medical note does not automatically waive attendance, live-session, or capstone requirements. A coordinator may inspect supporting evidence and check whether the attendance record contains an error. Attendance may be corrected only after the evidence is verified. Until then, assess the learner using the existing recorded value and explain that a correction is pending. Do not invent a corrected percentage.

## Makeup sessions

With mentor approval, one missed live session may be made up within 7 calendar days of the original session. The learner should identify the missed session date. A makeup request is not a completed session and does not immediately increase the session count. If the date or approval is unavailable, ask for it before confirming the makeup option. The approved session must actually be completed and recorded.

## Who decides

The coordinator verifies records; the mentor approves a permitted makeup; the learner provides missing evidence. The assistant recommends a next action and cites the policy. It does not grant approval, waive a threshold, or issue a certificate. A learner with confirmed unmet requirements is not currently eligible; a learner with unresolved facts should be marked pending.

## Illustration

A learner with 76% attendance and 2 sessions remains below the recorded requirements even if a medical note is offered. Another learner with 82%, 3 sessions, and an unknown capstone score has satisfied the attendance conditions, but the overall certificate decision is still pending.
